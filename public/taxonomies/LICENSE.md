# Taxonomy data licensing

`msc2020-codes.json` is derived from the MSC2020 classification jointly
published by Mathematical Reviews and zbMATH and is distributed under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
The snapshot was retrieved from [MSC2020](https://msc2020.org/) on 2026-08-01
and includes the description corrections recorded in PalomarSubmission commit
`6d9175a6e10fe33124e8ea9e053b494f9fa6a41d`.

`arxiv-categories.json` is derived from
[arXiv's public category taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/category_taxonomy), also
retrieved on 2026-08-01.

These third-party terms apply to the taxonomy snapshots independently of the
surrounding Palomar Server software.
